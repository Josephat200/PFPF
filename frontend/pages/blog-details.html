What makes a portfolio feel like a product?

A portfolio is not only an archive. It is an invitation to understand how you think.

The best portfolio pages reduce friction. They explain what was built, why it mattered, and what you learned. They make the next step obvious without overwhelming the reader.

That is the standard I am working toward with Josphat Labs: a small, clear, honest window into the work behind the work.
